# fender8891's Target Dummy

Stop guessing how hard your weapons hit.

**fender8891's Target Dummy** is a compact combat-testing mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge**. Place a reusable dummy, choose what you want to test against, and get immediate health, armor, and damage feedback without chasing mobs around your base.

## Three Testing Modes

### Immortal Steve

The classic test dummy has 100 health, stands still, ignores knockback, and restores itself instead of dying. Equip it through its GUI with armor, a main-hand item, and an off-hand item.

Choose a custom loadout or instantly test against complete armor sets:

- Leather
- Chainmail
- Iron
- Gold
- Diamond
- Netherite

### Passive Mob Simulation

Replace Steve with a real selectable vanilla mob. Simulated mobs use their normal vanilla health, armor, collision, gravity, damage handling, and other attributes, but have no AI. Kill one and it automatically respawns for another test.

Available passive mobs:

## Combat Information Card

The optional floating information card shows:

- Current and maximum health
- A visual health bar
- Current armor points
- A visual armor bar
- Damage from the most recent hit

Toggle the card directly from the dummy GUI.

## Easy to Use

- Place the Target Dummy item in the world.
- Right-click Steve or the selected simulated mob to open the GUI.
- Choose Immortal Steve, Passive Mobs, or Hostile Mobs.
- Select a mob and start testing.
- Sneak-right-click to pack everything up and recover the Target Dummy and any installed equipment.

## Crafting Recipe

Craft the Target Dummy with:

- 6 Paper
- 1 Glass
- 1 Armor Stand
- 1 Redstone Block

Arrange the ingredients as follows:

```text
Paper         Paper          Paper
Glass         Armor Stand    Redstone Block
Paper         Paper          Paper
```

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- No other mods required

Whether you are comparing enchantments, balancing a modpack, measuring armor penetration, or simply answering "how hard does this thing hit?", fender8891's Target Dummy gives you a repeatable target without the usual setup hassle.
